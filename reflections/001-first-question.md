# First Question

At the beginning of PEMTSbrothers, an important question emerged:

How do we build governance and technical systems
that remain calm, ethical, and useful
even as they grow beyond their creators?

This question arose before formal decisions,
before authority structures,
and before implementation.

It reflects an awareness that systems tend to drift
toward speed, control, and accumulation
unless intentionally guided otherwise.

This question does not demand an immediate answer.
It exists to be revisited
as the institution learns, grows, and adapts.
