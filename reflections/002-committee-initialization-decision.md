# Reflection: Decision 002 – Committee Framework Initialization

## Context
Following the establishment of the initial governance structure of PEMTSbrothers, there arose a clear need to define how responsibility, oversight, and collective stewardship would be distributed. This reflection records the institutional meaning of the decision to initialize committees.

## Decision Summary
PEMTSbrothers formally adopted a committee-based framework to support governance, decision review, advisory functions, and ethical oversight. Committees are designed to serve, not dominate, and to act as custodians of process rather than centers of power.

## Rationale
Committees were introduced to:
- Prevent concentration of authority
- Encourage collective wisdom and accountability
- Allow specialization without fragmentation
- Ensure continuity beyond individual participation

This decision aligns with the founding principle that PEMTSbrothers exists for generational benefit and shared responsibility.

## Institutional Insight
Committees are not permanent authorities. They are living structures that may evolve, dissolve, or be reformed as the institution grows. Their legitimacy flows from service, transparency, and adherence to shared values.

## Forward Note
Future reflections should document how committees function in practice, including lessons learned, adaptations made, and moments where restraint proved as important as action.
