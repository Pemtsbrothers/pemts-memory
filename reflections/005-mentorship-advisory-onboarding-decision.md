# Decision Record 005 – Mentorship and Advisory Onboarding

**Proposal:** 005 – Mentorship and Advisory Onboarding  
**Decision Date:** [Insert Date]  
**Decision Implemented By:** Governance Committee / PEMTSbrothers Leadership

## Summary
The mentorship and advisory onboarding proposal was approved. Key points:

- Onboarding follows the process described in Proposal 005.  
- Orientation materials must reference PEMTSbrothers governance principles and institutional memory.  
- Annual feedback and engagement reports are mandatory and stored in the institutional memory.  
- Each mentor/advisor is assigned a point of contact to ensure proper integration.  
- Future process adjustments must remain consistent with governance principles.

## Purpose
To ensure mentorship and advisory activities are aligned with governance, auditable, and sustainable.
